# Unibots UK
## Season 2026 / 2027 - The Laboratory

This is a repository to track the development and creation of Edge Hill University's autonomous robot attempt for the Unibots UK 2027 competition dubbed 'The Laboratory'.


## Competition challenge Information
The task of this competition is to create a fully autonomous robot that can navigate an arena and gather metal ball bearing and small 3D printed icosahedron shapes and then deposit them into a goal area all while avoiding other teams robots within the arena.


## Match description
•	Each match is 180seconds long (3 mins).

•	Arena is 2500mm x 2500mm and walls are 157mm high. 

•	4 match colours: yellow, orange, purple and green.

•	Arena contains 45 total game pieces: 24 stell balls (atoms 20mm) and 21 icosahedrons (compounds 30mm), 7 red, 7 green, and 7 blue.

•	Robot must be fully automatic and start in assigned team area touching the wall.


## Point system
• Park robot sucsesfully at the end	- 5 points

• End match holding an atom (holding even when robot lifted) - 1 point per atom

• Score atom in net	- 2 points per atom

• Score compound in net	- 4 points per compound

• Score a tri-compound set in net (1 red, 1 green, 1 blue) - 4 points per tri-compound



## Robot main design restrictions
•	Size – 200mm x 200mm but can autonomously expand to 300mm x 300mm during the match.

•	Autonomy – Robot must be entirely autonomous with a physical start button and power switch.

•	No interference -  Robot must avoid collisions and NOT intentionally disrupt the match.

•	Safety – NO weapons, sharp edges, loose wires or exposed battery. Battery must be secure and safely removed, charged and stored.

•	Identification -  Robot must have a clearly visible team number sign at least 50mm x 50mm.

•	Launch mechanism – Any launch mechanism must be hardware limited to a safe level and within a 2-meter launch distance.
